开源可穿戴的 AI 工牌是 go-voice 唯一的收音设备：内置 ES8311 音频编解码器，通过蓝牙把语音实时传给 Mac，屏幕显示连接与收音状态。 The open, wearable AI badge is the only capture device for go-voice. Its ES8311 codec streams your voice to the Mac over Bluetooth while the screen shows connection and capture status.

  • UP开始 / 结束说话Start / stop speaking
  • DOWN发送回车Send Enter
  • OK删除或取消，长按清空Delete or cancel, hold to clear

工牌需运行 Go Voice 固件，可在 go-voice 的“固件安装”页通过 USB 写入，设备身份与永久 Recovery 保持不变。 The badge runs Go Voice firmware, which go-voice can install over USB from its Firmware page while keeping device identity and permanent Recovery intact.

三步，从声音到文字Voice to text in three steps

  1. 01

    按键说话Press and talk

    按下工牌 UP 键开始收音，go-voice 记住你当前所在的应用。Press UP on the badge to start. go-voice remembers the app you are in.

  2. 02

    本机识别Recognize locally

    Apple 本机语音识别边说边出字，按词库与替换规则纠正专有名词。Apple on-device recognition shows words as you speak and fixes names with your vocabulary and replacements.

  3. 03

    插入光标Insert at cursor

    可选 AI 润色去掉语气词、修正标点，然后直接插入目标应用。Optional AI refinement removes fillers and fixes punctuation, then the text goes straight into your app.

为听写而生的细节Built for dictation

原生 macOS 菜单栏应用，基于 MyGo 构建，界面固定暗色。 A native macOS menu-bar app built with MyGo, in a fixed dark interface.

本机实时转写Live on-device transcription

默认使用 Apple SpeechAnalyzer 在 Mac 本机识别；也可明确选择支付宝 MaaS 在线模型。Apple SpeechAnalyzer runs on your Mac by default. Alipay MaaS online models are available when you choose them.

悬浮听写胶囊Floating dictation capsule

屏幕底部的胶囊实时滚动显示识别文字，可随时取消或跳过润色直接插入。A capsule at the bottom of the screen scrolls live text and lets you cancel or skip refinement at any time.

词库与替换词Vocabulary & replacements

注入专有名词，按拼音找出同音错字；替换规则在本地直接生效，支持 JSON 导入导出。Inject your terms and catch homophones by pinyin. Replacement rules apply locally, with JSON import and export.

AI 润色AI refinement

Apple 本地模型或 OpenAI 兼容接口，提供专名、轻度、结构化模式；失败时保留识别原文。Apple's local model or any OpenAI-compatible API, with names-only, light and structured modes. Failures keep the original text.

USB 固件安装USB firmware install

内置签名固件，写入前逐项预检，只写应用分区，不提供整片擦除。Bundled signed firmware with strict preflight checks. Only the application partition is written; there is no erase-all.

常驻菜单栏 · 自动更新Menu bar · Auto-update

可登录时启动；正式版经过签名与公证，每天检查一次 Ed25519 签名更新。Opens at login if you like. Release builds are signed and notarized and check daily for Ed25519-signed updates.

你的声音，留在你手里Your voice stays yours

默认全部在本机完成。任何云端处理都需要你明确选择。 Everything runs on your Mac by default. Any cloud processing requires your explicit choice.

  • 不用 Mac 麦克风No Mac microphone只接收你所选工牌的音频，应用不申请麦克风权限。Audio comes only from the badge you select; the app never requests microphone access.
  • 不保存音频No saved audio音频只在内存中处理，不保存、不回放，也不保留转写历史。Audio is processed in memory only. It is never saved or replayed, and there is no transcript history.
  • 密钥只存钥匙串Keys live in KeychainAPI key 仅存入 macOS 钥匙串，不写入设置文件或日志。API keys are stored only in the macOS Keychain, never in settings files or logs.
  • 不悄悄上云No silent cloud fallback本地识别或润色失败时保留原文，绝不自动改用云端。If local recognition or refinement fails, the original text is kept; nothing switches to the cloud on its own.
  • 云端功能透明可控Cloud features you control选择在线识别时发送本次语音音频；选择远端润色时只发送转写文字与词库，不发送音频。Online recognition sends session audio. Remote refinement sends only the transcript and vocabulary, never audio.
